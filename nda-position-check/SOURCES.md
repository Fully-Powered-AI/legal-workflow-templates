# Sources and credits

Written by Fully Powered AI in its own words. Three ideas come from the NDA triage method in anthropics/claude-for-legal (Apache-2.0): judge an NDA only against a lawyer's written positions for the company's side; never fill a gap in those positions with a default; and send any NDA that binds a party to more than confidentiality to a lawyer. Stopping when positions for that side are missing or were not set by a lawyer, the range of NDA topics such positions may cover, and not telling the other side the company will sign before a lawyer has decided also draw on it. No text from it is reused. Fully Powered AI left out its outcome that routes a clean NDA to signature: even a full match is a draft addressed to counsel, nothing is routed to signature, and only counsel decides and signs. Its own additions are the comparison that quotes every clause beside the position it was measured against, per-clause marks, a fixed rule for what counts as a difference, a line for each position the NDA does not address, a confirm line for each defined term read as an ordinary word, flags that name their list item word for word, and the review-date check on the positions file. No NDA positions come with the workflow template.
