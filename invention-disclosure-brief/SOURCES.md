One part comes from the invention intake screen in the IP plugin of anthropics/claude-for-legal (Apache-2.0): before anything else, get the inventor to say in one round what the invention is, how it works, who worked on it and when, and every occasion it has been shown, sold or offered outside the company, naming each kind of occasion so none is forgotten; start from the company's own disclosure form where there is one; and close with the facts still open. That source goes on to screen the invention and recommend whether to pursue it. Fully Powered AI left all of that out and added the rest: a brief built only from the company's own records, every statement quoted and located; the contributor list copied from the records, with anyone named elsewhere set beside it; one dated record of events with every disagreement between records shown; each outside occasion copied from the company's log with its audience and the log's own note on any confidentiality agreement; papers and products named in the records marked for the lawyer to verify; patent counsel's own questions answered only from the records; the list of missing records; and the lines counsel confirms. No date is counted. No third-party text is reused.
