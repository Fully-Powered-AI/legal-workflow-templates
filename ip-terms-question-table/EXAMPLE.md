# IP ownership and licence question table: an invented example

*Illustrative example with invented data.* Quillfern Labs, Inc., Tenmoor Studio LLC, the State of Westmarch, the people, the agreement, the two statements of work and the IP positions file below are all invented. The positions file is one invented company's way of setting out its lawyer's positions. It is not a real company's playbook, it says nothing about any real law, and it must not be used on a real agreement.

The contracts manager's inputs come first, in full, then what the workflow template prepares. The template asks for four inputs; here the documents are shown one by one, as inputs 2 to 4, and today's date is given in the request, so they appear as five.

## Input 2: the agreement

```
MASTER SERVICES AGREEMENT
Between Quillfern Labs, Inc. ("Client") and Tenmoor Studio LLC ("Agency")
Effective Date: 1 December 2026
Version sent by Agency, 3 November 2026. Unsigned.

1. Definitions
1.1 "Confidential Information" means non-public information a party
discloses to the other in connection with this Agreement and marks or
identifies as confidential.
1.2 "Deliverables" means the materials Agency prepares for Client under a
Statement of Work and identifies there as deliverables.
1.3 "Agency Materials" means the tools, templates, code, component kits and
know-how that Agency held before the Effective Date or develops apart from
this Agreement, including those listed in Schedule B.
1.4 "Statement of Work" means a document signed by both parties that
describes services and Deliverables and refers to this Agreement.

2. Services
2.1 Agency will perform the services described in each Statement of Work.
2.2 Client will supply the brand assets and product information Agency
reasonably requests.

3. Fees and payment
3.1 Client will pay the fees in each Statement of Work within 30 days of a
correct invoice.

4. Client materials
4.1 Client grants Agency a non-exclusive, non-transferable licence to use
Client's brand assets and product information solely to perform the
services during the term.

5. Personnel
5.1 Agency is responsible for its personnel and any subcontractors and will
ensure each has agreed in writing to terms consistent with Sections 7 and 9.

6. Term and termination
6.1 This Agreement runs for two years from the Effective Date. Either party
may end it on 60 days' written notice.
6.2 Sections 7 and 9 survive termination.

7. Intellectual property
7.1 Upon payment in full of the fees for a Statement of Work, Agency agrees
to assign to Client all right, title and interest in the Deliverables under
that Statement of Work.
7.2 Agency retains all rights in Agency Materials. Where Agency Materials
are included in a Deliverable, Agency grants Client a non-exclusive,
perpetual, worldwide, royalty-free licence to use them as part of that
Deliverable.
7.3 Agency may show the Deliverables in its portfolio once Client has
released them publicly.

8. Warranties
8.1 Agency warrants that the Deliverables will not, to its knowledge,
infringe any third party's intellectual property rights.

9. Confidentiality
9.1 Each party will keep the other's Confidential Information confidential
and use it only for this Agreement, during the term and for three (3) years
after it ends.
9.2 On request at the end of the term, each party will return or destroy
the other's Confidential Information.
9.3 Nothing in this Agreement restricts either party from using ideas,
concepts and know-how retained in the unaided memory of its personnel.

10. Limitation of liability
10.1 Neither party is liable for indirect or consequential loss.

11. General
11.1 This Agreement is governed by the laws of the State of Westmarch.
11.2 If a Statement of Work conflicts with this Agreement, this Agreement
controls unless the Statement of Work states expressly that it overrides a
named section of this Agreement.
```
