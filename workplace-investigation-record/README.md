# Workplace investigation record for counsel

When counsel directs a workplace investigation, the HR investigator turns the interview notes and documents into one restricted record for that lawyer: the allegations, who said what, a dated chronology, where accounts differ, an evidence index and the follow-up still open, each pinned to its source.

*Workflow template · Legal and contracts · version 0.1.0 · [Workflows Directory](https://fullypowered.ai/workflows/workplace-investigation-record/)*

## The outcome

Counsel opens one record instead of a folder of interview notes, message screenshots and rotas. Every allegation is there in the words counsel used to frame it, with the passage of the complaint it came from. Every statement is quoted from the notes and attributed to the person who made it, with the interview and paragraph beside it. The dated occasions run in order, each listing the statements and documents that mention it. Where two people, or a person and a document, give different accounts of the same point, the accounts sit side by side and stay unresolved.

The record is careful where investigation files usually slip. An allegation is never rewritten as something that happened. A witness repeating what someone else told them is marked as second-hand. "Around the start of April" is not turned into a date. Words that judge an act, such as harassed, threatened or retaliated, appear only inside someone's quoted words. The interviewer's own impressions of an interviewee, and health or family details the allegations do not concern, are kept out and listed for counsel to read in the notes.

It is preparation, and it stays preparation. The workflow template gives no legal advice. It reaches no conclusion about what happened, who is believed, whether any policy or law was breached or what the law requires, and it decides nothing about legal meaning, merit, validity, privilege, conflicts or deadlines. It writes no investigation memo, report, outcome letter or warning, even as a draft for counsel to edit. It sends, files, signs, shares and calendars nothing. It names no law or case of its own; one that an interviewee mentions stays inside their quoted words, marked for the lawyer to verify. The label at the top is printed because counsel asked for it, and the record says plainly that printing it is not a privilege decision. Asked who is telling the truth, whether something was harassment or what to do about anyone, it names counsel and points to the line of the record where counsel decides, and says nothing more.

## Who starts the work, and when

The HR investigator working under counsel's direction starts it, once interviews have been held and documents collected, and runs it again after each further round. In a company with a legal team, a paralegal may run it for in-house employment counsel. Where the company has no lawyer of its own, the named counsel is the outside counsel directing the investigation, and the record is addressed to them, never to the business as an answer. With no lawyer named at all, it prepares nothing and asks who will direct the investigation.

The record goes to the named counsel and to no one else on the access list until counsel has signed it.

## What they supply

- **Counsel's instruction:** the matter, the named counsel, the scope period, the allegations in counsel's words and numbered, the complaint document, the participants and their roles, the access list and the label counsel wants printed.
- **The interview records,** one per interview, each with a short header (who, role, date, interviewer, note-taker, whether the notes were read back) and numbered paragraphs, each marked with the allegation the question was about.
- **The evidence log,** exported with the column headings the template names. If a heading does not match, the workflow template stops and asks how the columns match.
- **The documents themselves,** every page, labelled with the log's IDs, with message exports keeping their message numbers.
- **The earlier version of the record,** when it is being rebuilt after more interviews.

## What comes out

One record, headed as restricted and as a draft for the named counsel, saying it gives no legal advice, reaches no conclusion and drafts no memo, report or letter:

- **Allegations in scope,** in counsel's words, with where each was raised and the statements and differences that concern it.
- **Attributed statements,** every substantive paragraph of the notes quoted, numbered and located, with second-hand accounts and any law or rule an interviewee mentions marked.
- **Chronology:** each occasion a source dates exactly, in order, with every source that mentions it, and a list of the statements that place things in time only loosely.
- **Disputed facts:** each point on which accounts differ, every account quoted and located, ending "Not resolved here."
- **Evidence index:** every interview record and every document, with pages read, where each is used and any legend copied as printed.
- **Open follow-up:** people named as present who were not interviewed, records referred to but not collected, things an interviewee offered to send, notes not read back, and new matters an interviewer marked, each left for counsel to decide.
- **Not carried:** what was kept out of the record and where it sits in the notes.
- **Counsel to confirm:** a line for each point counsel must check or decide, from each difference between accounts to findings, credibility, any memo or letter, and who may receive the record.
- **Check:** the counts, so counsel can see nothing was dropped.

An empty section says "None." The HR investigator spot-checks the record against the notes and gives it to the named counsel.

## What has to change for your company

Counsel and HR agree the conventions once. The interview notes need a header and numbered paragraphs, with each paragraph marked by the allegation the question was about and a mark for anything new the interviewee raised. Fix how your evidence log's columns map to the template's headings. Decide who may be named counsel, who sets the access list and the label, and where the confirmed record is kept. If counsel wants more kinds of personal detail kept out, or more judging words kept inside quotes, add them.

We sit with your HR investigators and the counsel who directs their work, write down how investigation records are really kept today, and set it up in the AI product your company has approved: ChatGPT, Claude, Copilot or Gemini. Check with counsel and your own policy that interview notes and investigation documents may be used in it before any go in. Your company keeps control of how the work gets done.
