# Legal hold custodian tracker

Once counsel has issued a legal hold, your legal operations lead keeps one tracker for counsel: every custodian and system in counsel’s scope, who has acknowledged and who is overdue, who has left or changed role, the refresh date counsel set, and reminders copied from counsel’s own text for a person to send.

*Workflow template · Legal and contracts · version 0.1.0 · [Workflows Directory](https://fullypowered.ai/workflows/legal-hold-custodian-tracker/)*

## The outcome

Once counsel has issued a hold, someone has to keep track of it week after week: who has acknowledged, who has not, and who has left or changed role since the notice went out, across many people and systems. With this workflow template, the person who keeps the hold record sees the whole picture on one page, every week, in the same shape: each person and each system counsel named, the day each notice went out, who has acknowledged, and who is past the proposed due date and by how many days. The moment the HR export shows someone on counsel’s record leaving or moving to a new role, the tracker puts it in front of counsel, saying where counsel’s record lists that person, with the offboarding checklist’s dates for mailbox removal and laptop wipes beside it, before those dates arrive rather than after.

Reminders are drafted for a person to send, copied word for word from the text counsel approved, only for the people counsel’s own rule allows and only where nothing else about that person is waiting for counsel. Anyone who has changed role, is leaving or does not match the record gets no reminder until counsel decides. If counsel’s reminder rule does not say plainly who sends, from when and how many, no reminder is drafted and the rule goes back to counsel, quoted. If the tracker is not ready, because the HR export is missing or out of date, the hold record is older than the log, or a date cannot be read, no reminder is drafted for anyone. The tracker goes to counsel first, and the drafted reminders go out only after counsel has confirmed the proposed due dates. Everything else arrives as a short list of decisions for counsel, each with its facts.

It is a tracker, and it stays one. It has not been reviewed by a lawyer. It gives no legal advice. It decides nothing about legal meaning, merit, validity, privilege, conflicts or deadlines. It sends, files, signs and calendars nothing. Counsel writes, issues, changes and releases the hold and decides its scope; the tracker never drafts or sends the hold notice, never decides who is a custodian, and never deletes, releases or moves any data. A date it counts, such as when an acknowledgement falls due, is a proposed date, counted only under counsel’s written rule with the working shown. Every line in the tracker is copied, located or counted from counsel’s record and the exports, or is a question for counsel. Asked for a view, a verdict, a deadline, a case or what to do, it says only that the question is for the named lawyer and adds it to the tracker as a question for that lawyer, unanswered.

## Who starts the work, and when

The legal operations lead or the paralegal who owns preservation runs it after counsel has issued a hold, then each week until counsel releases it, and again whenever the HR export shows someone on the record leaving or changing role. The lawyer who issued the hold decides every open point: in-house counsel, or, where the company has no lawyer of its own, the outside counsel who issued it, to whom the tracker is then addressed. It is never an answer for the business to act on.

It does not start a hold. If no lawyer has issued one, it prepares nothing.

## What they supply

- **Counsel’s hold record.** The hold reference, the matter as counsel describes it, who issued the hold and when, the custodians and systems in scope with their owners, the refresh date counsel set, counsel’s written rule for when an acknowledgement is due, counsel’s rule on reminders, and the reminder text counsel approved.
- **The acknowledgement log,** exported with fixed column headers: who each notice went to, when it was sent, when it was acknowledged and how many reminders have gone.
- **The HR export** for the people on the record: status, job title, the date of any change and any last working day.
- **The offboarding checklist** for anyone on the record who is leaving, when one exists.
- **Today’s date.**

If an export’s headers are not the ones expected, the tracker stops and asks how the columns match, rather than guessing.

## What comes out

One tracker, headed as a draft for the lawyer who issued the hold:

- **Custodians and systems,** in counsel’s order, each with sent and acknowledged dates, a proposed due date with its working where one is still needed, and a status.
- **Overdue acknowledgements,** oldest proposed due date first, each saying whether a reminder is drafted, has been held back for counsel, or has reached counsel’s reminder limit.
- **People changes** from the HR export, with where counsel’s record lists each person, the offboarding checklist’s lines and dates copied as written, and no view on which of them the hold covers.
- **Record mismatches:** anyone in the log who is not on counsel’s record, and any name or ID that does not agree.
- **The refresh date** counsel set, and how far away it is.
- **Draft reminders** for the legal operations lead to send from their own mailbox once counsel has confirmed the proposed due dates, or none at all when the tracker is not ready.
- **Counsel to decide or confirm:** one line for each point, then a check block of what was read.

When a section has nothing in it, it says “None.”

## What has to change for your company

Counsel writes the hold record, and it is the heart of this. Counsel sets the rule for when an acknowledgement is due, the reminder text, who may send a reminder, from when and how many times before the person comes back to counsel, and the refresh date. The template supplies none of these and invents none. Legal operations sets the column headers of the acknowledgement log and agrees the HR export with the people team. You name who checks the tracker, where counsel’s decisions are recorded, and how often it runs.

We sit with the person who keeps your holds and the lawyer who issues them, write down what is really done, and set it up in the AI product your company has approved: ChatGPT, Claude, Copilot or Gemini. Your company keeps control of how the work gets done.
