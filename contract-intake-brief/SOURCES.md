One idea comes from the contract review entry point in the commercial legal plugin of anthropics/claude-for-legal (Apache-2.0): tell what an agreement is from the title on its first page and the title of each exhibit and schedule before reading any of its clauses, and treat those titles, not words in the body, as the signal for where the agreement goes, so that a long services agreement stamped confidential on every page is not routed as a confidentiality agreement, and have a person check that reading before the work goes further. Fully Powered AI added the rest: the comparison of the attachments the clauses mention with the ones that actually arrived; the parties copied from the opening, each signature block and each attachment, with every difference set out for counsel; the signature blocks recorded as found; the requester's purpose and date kept in the requester's own words; routing only under the company's own counsel-approved rules, shown rule by rule; the factual questions for the requester; the lines counsel confirms; and the rule that the brief reads no term for its meaning. No third-party text is reused.
