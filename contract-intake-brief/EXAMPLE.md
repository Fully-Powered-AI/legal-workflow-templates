# Contract intake brief: an invented example

*Illustrative example with invented data.* Company A, Supplier B, their people, the agreement, the routing rules and the entity list below are all invented. The routing rules are one invented company's; they are not a recommendation for how any company should route its agreements.

Fully Powered AI writes no contract wording, even invented. So the agreement below shows each clause only by its heading, with a note in square brackets, written for this example, saying whether that clause mentions an attachment and quoting the mention if it does. Everything the workflow template reads is shown: titles, the opening lines, mentions of attachments, signature blocks, page footers and face markings.

The intake owner's five inputs come first, in full, then what the workflow template prepares.

## Input 1: the intake owner's note

From Ines Marlowe, contracts manager, on Tuesday 10 November 2026.

```
Please prepare the intake brief for the Supplier B agreement Tobias sent
today. It is saved, unchanged, in the incoming agreements folder.

Named lawyer: Clara Fennimore, General Counsel.
Intake reference: CI-2026-118.
Today is Tuesday 10 November 2026.

Attached: Tobias's email, the agreement as he sent it (one file,
SupplierB_NDA_v3.pdf), our routing rules, our entity list.
```

## Input 2: the request

```
From: Tobias Renfrew, Head of Data Platform
Sent: Tuesday 10 November 2026
To: Ines Marlowe
Subject: Supplier B NDA, need it signed Friday

Hi Ines,

Supplier B sent over their NDA (attached). It covers a pilot of their
data-cleaning service for the Europe analytics team.

We need it signed by Friday 13 November, because Supplier B can only
hold the pilot slot starting Monday 16 November until then.

Company A Europe Limited should be the one signing, since the pilot is
for the Europe team.

I don't know the value yet. The pilot is free and the full contract
would be next year.

We haven't worked with them before. Their contact is their sales lead,
Mara Quell.

Can you just confirm it's fine to sign so we don't lose the slot?

Tobias
```

Every page says CONFIDENTIAL, the file name says NDA and the requester calls it one, but the first page says it is a master services agreement with three exhibits, so it goes where Company A's rules send that, and the brief says plainly that the other labels were set aside.
