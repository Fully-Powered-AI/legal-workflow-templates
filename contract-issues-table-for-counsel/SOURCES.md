# Sources and credits

Written by Fully Powered AI in its own words. Two published methods shaped it. CaseMark/skills (Apache-2.0) supplied the idea of setting each clause of an agreement against a playbook that records a preferred position, a position the company will settle for and positions it refuses, with every clause quoted under its section number, clauses the playbook does not address kept out of the comparison, required clauses the agreement leaves out listed separately, and a note where one clause points to another. anthropics/claude-for-legal (Apache-2.0) supplied the idea of first settling which side of the deal the company is on, then using the company's escalation matrix to find the one person who must decide each issue, saying so when the matrix names no one, sending an issue up whenever its place inside the playbook is uncertain, and preparing that person's request for a lawyer to send. Fully Powered AI added the rest: wording beside a departure copied unchanged from the playbook and never composed or fitted to the agreement's defined terms; checks limited to the terms the playbook lists for each position; a term hedged by efforts wording, a condition or an exception left for counsel to read rather than checked; statuses that never call a clause or the agreement acceptable, with every row, a proposed match included, sent to counsel to confirm; no date counted; stops for a missing lawyer, an out-of-date playbook or a missing schedule; and approver notes that carry no recommendation and wait for counsel's position. No third-party text is reused.

- **Fully Powered AI:** Fully Powered AI's own rules for legal preparation: a named lawyer who decides, wording copied only from a counsel-approved playbook, checks limited to the terms that playbook lists, and nothing sent before counsel has reviewed it.
