# Sources and credits

Written by Fully Powered AI in its own words. The core of the work comes from a published method in anthropics/claude-for-legal (Apache-2.0): choosing which open matters are due a check-in by how long the firm has been silent and how close the next date is, one email per matter covering progress, dates, open decisions and fees against the approved budget, review by counsel before anything goes, a closing list of matters drafted and passed over, flags for matters with no outside firm (raised when the matter is high risk) and for matters with a firm but no email address, leaving out an empty numbered item and renumbering the rest, keeping to broad questions when the notes are thin, and the reminder that counsel reads each check-in for strategy or privileged content before it goes. Fully Powered AI added the fixed export columns that stop the run when they do not match, the ordered selection rules, the rule that every item is a question, a separate internal section of the notes that never reaches an email, the fee sums with their working, and the lines counsel confirms on each draft and on the summary. No third-party text is reused.
